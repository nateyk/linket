A link-in-bio page gives you one URL that collects everything you share — social profiles, products, articles — behind a single link that works nicely on mobile.

Build your page

  1. Go to your Link pages section and create a new page.
  2. Pick a title, handle, and avatar so the page feels like yours.
  3. Add links one by one. Each link can have its own title, icon, and thumbnail.
  4. Reorder them by dragging, so the most important links sit at the top.

Tips that help

  • Keep the first three links focused — most visitors never scroll further.
  • Use button styles that match your brand colors.
  • Share the page URL everywhere you have a bio field: social profiles, email signature, video descriptions.

When you are happy with the layout, publish the page — changes go live as soon as you save them.
