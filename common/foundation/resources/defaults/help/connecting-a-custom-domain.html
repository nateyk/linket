Connecting a custom domain takes a few minutes of setup plus a short wait for DNS to propagate. Here is the whole process end to end.

1. Add the domain

In Settings → Custom domains, click Add domain and enter the domain (or subdomain) you want to use, for example go.yourbrand.com.

2. Point your DNS

Your registrar gets two values from us:

  • A CNAME record pointing your subdomain at our servers.
  • A TXT record used to verify that you own the domain.

Every registrar labels these slightly differently, but the values we show you are always the same — copy them exactly.

3. Verify

Once the records are in place, click Verify. Most domains verify within minutes; some registrars take up to an hour.

Troubleshooting

  • Records not picked up yet? DNS changes can take up to 24 hours to propagate worldwide.
  • Double-check there is no conflicting CNAME on the same subdomain.
  • See DNS records explained for a deeper dive.
