You are free to move between plans whenever your needs change. Here is exactly what happens when you do.

Upgrading

Upgrades apply immediately. If you switch mid-cycle, we only charge the difference for the remaining days — the extra amount shows up on your next invoice rather than as a separate charge now.

Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep what you paid for until it runs out. If you exceed the lower plan's limits, existing links keep working; you simply cannot create new ones above the limit until you clean up.

Monthly vs yearly

Yearly billing lowers the effective monthly price. You can switch between monthly and yearly at any time — the change applies from your next renewal.

What never changes

  • Your links and analytics stay intact across any plan change.
  • Custom domains you already connected remain configured.
  • Teammates keep their workspace access, subject to the new plan's member limit.
