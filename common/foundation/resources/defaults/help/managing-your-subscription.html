Your billing settings live next to your account settings and cover everything related to your plan and payment.

Change your plan

Go to Settings → Billing to see your current plan and the others available. Switching takes effect immediately; upgrades are prorated to your next invoice.

Update your payment method

Add or remove cards from the payment section. The most recently added card is used for renewals automatically.

Download invoices

Every payment produces an invoice you can download as a PDF from the invoice history — useful for expenses and bookkeeping.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel from the same screen. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, then drops to the free plan — none of your links or analytics are deleted.
