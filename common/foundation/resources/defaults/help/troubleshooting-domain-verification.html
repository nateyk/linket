If your domain has been sitting on "pending" or "unverified" for a while, one of the causes below is almost certainly the reason.

The records are wrong

Compare what is in your registrar's DNS panel with the values we gave you, character by character. The most common mistakes are a trailing dot, a typo in the subdomain, or pasting the verification value into the CNAME field.

Records point at the wrong place

The CNAME must point at the exact host we provided. If you already had a CNAME on that subdomain — for example from a previous service — remove it or the new one will never take effect.

DNS hasn't propagated yet

New records can take up to 24 hours to be visible everywhere. If you added the records less than a day ago, wait and try again.

You are editing the wrong DNS

Records must be added where your nameservers point. If your domain uses a service like Cloudflare for DNS, adding records at the registrar will have no effect.

Still stuck?

Open a support ticket with a screenshot of your DNS panel and the domain name — we can usually pinpoint the mismatch from there.
