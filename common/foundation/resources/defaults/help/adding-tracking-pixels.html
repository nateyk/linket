Tracking pixels let you measure conversions from your short links inside advertising platforms, so ad attribution stays accurate even when the click passes through your domain first.

Add a pixel to a link

  1. Open the link you want to track and go to Pixels.
  2. Choose your platform (Facebook, Google, or a custom pixel).
  3. Paste the pixel ID the platform gave you.
  4. Save — the pixel now fires on every click of that link.

Custom pixels

Need something else? A custom pixel accepts any image or script-based tracking ID and fires it alongside your analytics.

Good to know

  • Pixels fire when the visitor leaves for the destination, so they never slow down the redirect itself.
  • You can attach different pixels to different links and compare performance per campaign.
