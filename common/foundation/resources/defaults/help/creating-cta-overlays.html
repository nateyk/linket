A CTA overlay places a small banner or announcement on top of the destination page your links point to — useful for promoting a product, an event, or a signup without changing the destination itself.

Create an overlay

  1. Open a link and choose CTA overlay.
  2. Write a headline, a short message, and the button label.
  3. Set where the overlay appears (top or bottom) and how it behaves on mobile.
  4. Save and test the link in a new tab.

When overlays work well

  • Promoting your own product while sharing someone else's content.
  • Announcing a limited-time offer to everyone who clicks a link.
  • Driving traffic to a signup page from high-traffic external posts.

Keep the message short — overlays that read like an ad get ignored, overlays that read like a tip get clicked.
