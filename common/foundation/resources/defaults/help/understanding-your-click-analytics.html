Analytics turn every click into something you can learn from. Here is what each part of your link analytics means.

Clicks over time

The main chart shows unique and total clicks per day. A spike usually lines up with a post, newsletter, or campaign going out — use it to see which shares actually drove traffic.

Referrers

Shows where visitors came from: an Instagram bio, a newsletter, a QR code, or a search engine. If a referrer surprises you, that channel is worth paying attention to.

Countries and devices

Country and device breakdowns help you decide things like posting times, image sizes, and whether most of your audience is on desktop or mobile.

UTM parameters

If your destination URL carries UTM parameters, clicks are grouped by campaign, source, and medium — so you can compare channels side by side inside one report.

Reports update within a few minutes of each click, and longer ranges are available from the date picker at the top of the page.
