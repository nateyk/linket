Workspaces let teammates or clients share one set of links, folders, and analytics instead of working in silos.

Send an invite

  1. Open Workspace settings.
  2. Enter your teammate's email address and choose a role.
  3. Send the invite — they will receive a link to join.

Roles at a glance

  • Admin — full control, including billing and member management.
  • Member — can create and edit links within the workspace.
  • Viewer — read-only access to links and analytics.

Members see the workspace after signing in, and everything they create stays inside it. You can change or revoke access at any time from the same settings screen.
