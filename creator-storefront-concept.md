# 🏪 Creator Storefront — Concept

> A new feature that lets creators sell digital products directly from their link-in-bio page. Each creator can turn their bio page into a mini storefront.

---

## 🎯 The Big Idea

Linket currently lets creators build beautiful bio pages with links, widgets, and customization. The next step is letting them **sell digital products** directly on that same page — no external store needed.

Creators can upload digital files (ebooks, presets, templates, courses, music, software, etc.), set a price, and customers can buy instantly with one click. The payment happens right there on the bio page, and the customer gets an instant download link.

---

## 📦 Three Product Tiers

The feature works across all three existing plan levels, with different benefits for each:

### 🆓 Free Plan
- Sell up to **7 digital products**
- **10% platform commission** on each sale (admin earns $3 on a $30 sale)
- Basic sales overview in the dashboard
- Standard file upload size
- Manual payout requests

### ⭐ Pro Plan
- Sell up to **50 digital products**
- **5% platform commission** (lower fees)
- Detailed sales analytics and charts
- Larger file uploads
- Discount code support (run promotions)
- Order bump upsells (suggest add-ons at checkout)
- Faster payout schedule

### 💼 Business Plan
- **Unlimited digital products**
- **2% platform commission** (lowest fees)
- All Pro features
- Largest file size limit
- Custom branded checkout experience
- Priority customer support
- Weekly automatic payouts

---

## 💰 How Money Flows

```
Customer buys $30 product
         ↓
Platform takes commission based on plan
  • Free:    $3.00  (10%)
  • Pro:     $1.50  (5%)
  • Business $0.60  (2%)
         ↓
Creator gets the rest
  • Free:    $27.00
  • Pro:     $28.50
  • Business $29.40
         ↓
Creator requests payout when ready
```

The model is simple — the more a creator pays for their plan, the less commission we take. This incentivizes upgrading while keeping the free tier accessible.

## 🛒 What Creators Can Sell

Any digital file type:
- **Ebooks & PDFs** — guides, workbooks, recipes
- **Design assets** — templates, presets, fonts, graphics
- **Audio** — music, beats, sound effects, audiobooks
- **Video** — courses, tutorials, workshops
- **Software** — plugins, themes, code snippets
- **Creative work** — illustrations, photography, prints
- **Digital services** — consultation booking, coaching sessions

---

## 👀 What Creators Will See

A new **"Digital Products"** section in their dashboard where they can:
- Create and manage products
- Upload files and preview images
- Set prices and descriptions
- See sales and earnings
- Request payouts

A new **"Shop" widget** they can add to their bio page:
- Shows their products as a beautiful card grid
- Customers click to see details and buy
- Checkout happens in a modal right on the page

---

## 👀 What Customers Experience

1. Land on a creator's bio page
2. See a "Shop" section with product cards
3. Click a product → see description, preview, price
4. Click "Buy Now" → enter email (or login)
5. Pay with Stripe or PayPal
6. Instant download button appears
7. Email receipt with download link sent
8. All seamless, no leaving the page

---

## 👀 What Admins Manage

- Set commission rates per plan (already works with existing permission system)
- Approve or reject product listings
- Process creator payout requests
- View marketplace revenue reports
- Manage refunds and disputes

## 🌟 Why This Works

- **No monthly fees** for basic selling — just pay-per-sale commission
- **All-in-one** — bio page + store + analytics in one place
- **No redirects** — customers buy without leaving the page
- **Beautiful stores** — inherits all the biolink appearance options
- **Built-in audience** — creators already have their bio page traffic
